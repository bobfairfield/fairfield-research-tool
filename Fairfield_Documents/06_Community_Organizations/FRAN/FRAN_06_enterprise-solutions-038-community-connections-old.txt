Enterprise Solutions & Community Connections




Enterprise Solutions & Community Connections focuses on understanding how communication, coordination, and volunteer networks function across Fairfield. Current efforts center on discovery and collaborative planning to inform future tools, partnerships, and initiatives, as needed.

ES & CC Supporting Plan




The Enterprise Solutions & Community Connections (ES & CC) subcommittee focuses on strengthening the connective systems that support Fairfield’s long-term resilience. This work emphasizes understanding existing communication channels, volunteer capacity, and economic support structures, then identifying opportunities to better coordinate efforts, share resources, and inform future community and economic initiatives.

This subcommittee’s role is primarily discovery, alignment, and planning, with early implementation focused on mapping systems, convening partners, and testing coordination models.

Building a Thriving Economy




This focus area centers on understanding Fairfield’s existing workforce, education, and entrepreneurship landscape. Early efforts emphasize mapping programs, identifying gaps, and aligning partners to inform future strategies that support local businesses, innovation, and long-term economic vitality.

Community Connections




One focus area within Community Connections examines how residents access information, events, and resources across Fairfield. Initial work prioritizes understanding communication tools, preferences, and gaps in order to inform more coordinated, accessible, and community-driven information-sharing systems over time.

Volunteer Network Support




This focus area focuses on understanding and strengthening Fairfield’s volunteer ecosystem. Early efforts include assessing existing volunteer organizations, identifying service gaps, and exploring coordination models that could better connect people, skills, and community needs in the future.
