Smith’s Helping Hands highlights a conversation between Faith Reeves and Mindy Smith about the growing need for compassionate, community-based care in Fairfield. Mindy shares how she and her daughter founded Smith’s Helping Hands to support individuals who want to age in place with dignity through in-home care, companionship, and practical assistance. She also introduces a new downtown respite and activity space designed to give caregivers a break while offering seniors a welcoming, social environment that keeps both mind and body engaged.

 

 

Here’s a concise summary of the Smith’s Helping Hands | Community Dialogue with Faith Reeves and Mindy Smith video (with timestamps):

 

00:01 – Introduction: Faith Reeves interviews Mindy Smith about Smith’s Helping Hands, a home care and support service in Fairfield started by Mindy and her daughter ~6 years ago.

00:53 – Service Overview: The organization provides in-home care: assistance with bathing, cooking, cleaning, and daily tasks so clients can age in place with dignity.

01:53 – Community Need: Mindy emphasizes strong local demand—many prefer to remain at home rather than move into institutional care.

02:25 – How It Works: Families reach out, and Mindy meets with them for a free visit to assess needs and advise on safety improvements and care.

03:41 – Services Expanded: Care includes accompaniment to appointments, errands, laundry, and companionship—even for family members living out of state.

05:15 – New Facility Introduction: Mindy explains a new downtown facility opening soon that offers respite care with activities like games, arts, socializing, and light exercise.

06:36 – Program Benefits: The space provides social engagement and gentle group activities while giving caregivers a break.

08:12 – Logistics: Facility hours are generally 10 AM–4 PM with flexibility by appointment; first hour is free to try.

09:12 – Open House: Mindy invites everyone to a ribbon cutting and open house with the Chamber of Commerce to see the space and services.

10:00 – Importance in Community: Faith highlights the need for such services for individuals living alone and expresses support.

Local context: Smith’s Helping Hands now operates at 117 E. Burlington Ave., Fairfield, offering supervised respite care and social activities in a welcoming setting.
