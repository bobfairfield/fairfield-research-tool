FRAN Subcommittees

The Fairfield Resilient Action Network consists of 6 active subcommittees with unique focus areas.

Foodways

Strengthens Fairfield’s local food system by supporting farmers, improving access to fresh food, building farm-to-table partnerships, expanding community gardens, and developing infrastructure for aggregation, processing, and distribution.
Learn More >

Enterprise Solutions & Community Connections

Improves community connectivity by coordinating communications, supporting volunteer networks, enhancing information-sharing systems, and fostering a resilient local economy through collaboration, innovation, and shared resources.
Learn More >

Land Use

Advances forward-thinking planning through citywide land inventories, Environmental, Social, Economic, and Sustainable (ESES) policy tools, code modernization, and strategies for optimizing undeveloped spaces to support sustainable growth and community vitality. 
Learn More >

Private Land Stewardship

Supports landowners in improving urban water quality, adopting conservation practices, restoring habitats, and protecting biodiversity through education, partnerships, and site-specific nature-based solutions.  
Learn More >

Buildings & Energy

Promotes healthy, energy-efficient neighborhoods by advancing housing repairs, Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) development, building-code updates, trades workforce training, and strategies to strengthen residential and commercial buildings. 
Learn More >

Waste Management

Reduces landfill waste and strengthens wastewater resilience by promoting diversion, reuse, education, circular systems, youth career pathways, and partnerships that support innovative waste solutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FRAN?

The Fairfield Resilient Action Network (FRAN) is a community-driven nonprofit that coordinates, funds, and supports projects that strengthen Fairfield’s economic opportunity, social well-being, and environmental health through collaboration across six volunteer subcommittees.

What is the Resilient Action Plan?

The Resilient Action Plan is Fairfield’s community-built roadmap— developed over three years by 35+ volunteers—that outlines priority projects in food systems, housing, land stewardship, waste reduction, economic vitality, and coordinated communications. It is a living document, designed to evolve as projects advance and community needs change.

What challenges is FRAN trying to address?

FRAN focuses on the interconnected issues identified through three years of volunteer research and planning:

Housing quality and energy efficiency

Urban water quality and land stewardship

Local food system gaps and farm viability

Waste reduction and wastewater system resilience

Fragmented community communication and volunteer networks

Underutilized land and outdated policy tools

These challenges affect daily life in Fairfield, and FRAN exists to bring partners together to solve them in practical, community-centered ways.

Why now?

Fairfield is entering a critical implementation phase, and we must not lose momentum: with early projects already underway, FRAN is closing a funding gap needed to launch Phase 2 and sustain the volunteer-driven work already in motion.

How Your Donation Will Be Put to Work

How will my donation be used?

Your donation directly funds the implementation of Fairfield’s Resilient Action Plan—supporting volunteer-led projects, community education, matching funds for grants, and essential coordination across our six subcommittees. It also helps sustain the Sustainability Coordinator role, which provides the backbone support needed to move projects from ideas into action.

Why does FRAN need fundraising?

FRAN is a new nonprofit entering a critical implementation phase. Fundraising fills the immediate gap needed to launch early projects, maintain project coordination, and meet matching requirements for state and federal grants. Community support ensures that the momentum built over three years of volunteer effort can continue without interruption.

How close is FRAN to its $50,000 launch goal?

We are currently 41% of the way to our $50,000 goal, with $29,500 left to raise. Every gift brings us closer to fully launching Phase 2 implementation—and helps ensure Fairfield continues moving toward a more resilient, thriving future.

Is my donation tax-deductible, and can I give to a specific project?

Yes. FRAN has been incorporated as an Iowa public-benefit nonprofit and has receive Federal 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status. Your donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law. 

Launch Fund donations are not currently project specific. They support the essential startup capacity FRAN needs to implement the Resilient Action Plan and sustain early projects across all six subcommittees.
