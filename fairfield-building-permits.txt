Fairfield Building & Zoning Permits — How to Apply
Source: cityoffairfieldiowa.com/75/Building-Zoning and cityoffairfieldiowa.com/88/Permits-Fees
Last verified: April 2026

WHAT REQUIRES A PERMIT
Any of the following require a zoning or building permit from the City of Fairfield:
- Additions to an existing home or building
- New garages, detached or attached
- Decks, pergolas, porches, patios (raised)
- Fences of any kind
- Accessory buildings and outbuildings
- Portable sheds and gazebos
- Solar panels or solar arrays
- Demolition of any of the above
- New construction of any structure
- Temporary storage containers (allowed max 90 days during active construction with valid permit)

WHAT DOES NOT REQUIRE A PERMIT
- Ground-level patios with surface height no greater than 4 inches above grade

HOW TO APPLY FOR A PERMIT
1. Download the Zoning Permit / Building Permit Application (fillable PDF) from:
   cityoffairfieldiowa.com/75/Building-Zoning
2. Review the Builder's Checklist (PDF) available on the same page
3. Review Chapter 20 of the City Code for zoning requirements
4. Complete the application and submit it to the Planning & Zoning office in person or by contacting the office directly

SPECIAL PERMIT TYPES

Home Occupation Permit
A home occupation permit is required when running a business from a residential dwelling as a secondary use. The business must be operated by the occupant of the dwelling unit.

Lodging House Permit
Lodging houses are permitted in R-2 and R-3 residential districts and B-1 General Business district (not on the main floor) when authorized by the Board of Adjustment. An application must be submitted and approved.

Rental Housing Registration
All rental housing in Fairfield must be registered at City Hall. Contact the Planning & Zoning office for registration requirements.

Film, Photo, or Video Shoot Permit
An application must be submitted for any commercial film, photo, or video shoot conducted in Fairfield.

Street, Alley, or Sidewalk Blocking Permit
The City has established a permitting process for blocking streets, alleys, sidewalks, and other public property to promote public safety.

APPEALS AND VARIANCES
To appeal a decision of the Zoning Administrator, or to request a special use permit, variance, exception, or interpretation to sections of the zoning ordinance, contact the Board of Adjustment. The Board meets once a month as needed.

PLANNING COMMISSION
The Planning Commission makes recommendations to the city council regarding the comprehensive plan, annexations, zoning, site plans, and subdivision development. The Commission meets once a month as needed.

HOUSING TAX INCREMENT FINANCING (TIF)
Fairfield City Council adopted a Housing TIF Policy in December 2014 to provide adequate and affordable housing and create additional housing for Fairfield residents. Contact the Planning & Zoning office for details.
