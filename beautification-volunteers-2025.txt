Fairfield Beautification Commission Honors Top Volunteers of 2025

FAIRFIELD – The Fairfield Beautification Commission held its Second Annual Volunteer Appreciation Dinner on November 14, 2025, recognizing those who have done the most to enhance the city's appearance.

The organization handed out three awards at its first ceremony in 2024, but in 2025 the board more than doubled that to recognize seven individuals and businesses. The event was held at the Fairfield Golf & Country Club, with approximately 100 people in attendance. Commission chair Deborah Williamson served as master of ceremonies, presenting a detailed slideshow of the group's past activities and each award winner.

2025 Award Recipients:

Rose of the Year Award
Went to Aaron and Melanny Hirshberg for renovating the historic home at 107 W. Washington Ave., just south of Green Building Supply. The Hirshbergs live there and have converted the carriage house into an Airbnb. The commission bestowed 10 Rose Awards to property owners in 2025; the Hirshbergs were selected as Rose of the Year.

Organization of the Year
Went to Cambridge Investment Research, which volunteered at the commission's major events including the Spring Clean-up, and volunteered on at least three additional separate occasions. Williamson noted that 2025 saw a marked increase in businesses and civic groups proactively reaching out to volunteer, compared to 2024 when the commission had to seek volunteers out.

Sponsor of the Year
Went to Ron Haines, a member of the Fairfield Park and Recreation Foundation, for securing funds for a gator and water pump for the downtown. The park and rec department could not devote more time to watering baskets, but Haines secured foundation funding for those donations. Volunteers were then found to water every day of the week, relieving the city of that responsibility.

Artist of the Year
Went to Karla Christensen, who created mosaics in Heritage Alley north of Central Park and handled all fundraising for the project, finding sponsors to cover each $350 mosaic.

Weed Team Volunteer of the Year
Went to Mike Carr, for going above and beyond in cleaning the garden nodes around the square and sprucing up Central Park. The Weed Team is led by Bob Ferguson (now a commission board member), whose volunteers spray weeds with a non-toxic solution and dig them up throughout a nine-block section of downtown.

Youth Volunteer of the Year
Went to Leo Melvin, who rode on a gator early in the morning with his mother Theresa Rachele to water hanging baskets and pick up trash throughout the summer.

Volunteer of the Year
Went to Chaz Alley, a Weed Team member, by unanimous commission decision. "He's brilliant, generous, competent and all these amazing things," Williamson said. "Bob Ferguson befriended Chaz and they became best buddies, working even after dark."

Key facts:
- Commission chair: Deborah Williamson
- Weed Team leader: Bob Ferguson (commission board member)
- The Weed Team covers a nine-block section of downtown Fairfield
- Non-toxic weed treatment used throughout
- 2025 was the second annual volunteer appreciation dinner
- 2024 had 3 awards; 2025 expanded to 7 awards
- Event venue: Fairfield Golf & Country Club
- Approximately 100 attendees
